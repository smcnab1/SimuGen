# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

SimuGen is an open-source ChatGPT plugin for creating clinical simulation scenarios.

## Data handling

SimuGen does not operate its own backend service, user accounts, analytics platform or database. The plugin does not independently collect, transmit or store the contents of your conversations.

Use of ChatGPT remains subject to OpenAI's own privacy and data-handling terms.

## Patient and health information

SimuGen is designed for fictional educational simulation. Do not provide identifiable information about real patients or other individuals.

Scenario content should use synthetic training data only. SimuGen is designed not to request or generate genuine healthcare or government identifiers.

## Third-party services

The public SimuGen website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Links may direct users to GitHub, ChatGPT, OpenAI or authoritative healthcare resources. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when SimuGen's functionality or data handling changes. Material changes will be reflected in the project repository and public website.

## Contact

Questions can be raised through the [SimuGen GitHub repository](https://github.com/smcnab1/SimuGen/issues).

Do not include confidential, clinical or sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue.

# Educational Standards and Debriefing

Use this shared reference when designing a case, facilitating an encounter, debriefing or reviewing educational material. Select the standards relevant to the activity, profession and setting.

## Simulation standards

| Source | Application and verification |
| --- | --- |
| [ASPiH Standards 2023](https://aspih.org.uk/professional-practice/aspih-standards/) and [published framework](https://aspih.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/ASPiH-Standards-2023-CDN-Final.pdf) | UK-oriented simulation guidance. The numbered summary in the framework covers core values including safety and inclusion (1), alignment to learning needs and choice of modality (6–7), prebrief (10), facilitated reflection (12) and considerations for summative assessment (13). Use the relevant faculty and resource-management sections for training, staffing or delivery concerns. |
| [INACSL Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice](https://www.inacsl.org/healthcare-simulation-standards-of-best-practice-) | The fourth-edition overview identifies the 2025 revisions to Prebriefing, Facilitation, The Debriefing Process and Professional Integrity. Use the separately dated standards for Simulation Design, Outcomes and Objectives, interprofessional education, and Evaluation of Learning and Performance. The public overview supports mapping at domain level. Check the linked full publication for any criterion-level claim. |

For design and review, trace objectives to observable actions, the cues and resources that make those actions possible, and what will be discussed or observed. Use a clear prebrief, accessible participation and a planned debrief. Choose fidelity appropriate to the objective and offer reasonable adjustments without relying on stereotypes. For interprofessional learning, identify shared objectives and make roles and communication opportunities explicit.

## UK professional education

- [HCPC Standards of Education and Training, 2017 edition](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/resources/standards/standards-of-education-and-training/) apply to approved programmes for HCPC-regulated professions and are the current edition before the September 2027 transition. Relate programme considerations such as safe practice-based learning and assessment to the context only where applicable.
- [HCPC revised Standards of Education and Training, published 2026](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-relevant-to-education-and-training/set/revised-standards-of-education-and-training-2026/) take effect in September 2027. Until then, use them as a future-facing reference where requested. Verify the effective edition and any implementation details at the time of review.
- Check the relevant professional regulator and local policy for other professions or jurisdictions. Programme standards support educator judgement. They cannot establish a scenario's approval or an individual's competence on their own.

## How to map a finding

Name the material reviewed and the observed issue, then give the applicable source, edition or publication year, standard or confirmed criterion, and proposed remedy. Use exact criteria only when the public text or an authoritative copy can be checked. Where only an overview is available, map to the domain and say what requires further verification. Identify separately any educational good practice, clinical guideline, local requirement or reviewer judgement. Avoid implied accreditation, certification or verified institutional compliance.

## Reflective debriefing

[Eppich and Cheng, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25710312/) describe PEARLS as a blend of learner self-assessment, focused facilitation and direct feedback or teaching. Use those strategies within reactions, description, analysis and transfer. A focused advocacy-inquiry turn connects an observed action to a concern and asks how the learner understood the situation. A brief plus-delta turn asks what to keep and what to change. Select methods to suit the learner and the evidence rather than administering a fixed script.

Use these approaches as educational guidance. A text-based debrief supports reflection and faculty discussion. Formal assessment and framework validation require separate evidence and oversight.
